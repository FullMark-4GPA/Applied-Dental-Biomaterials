Investment Materials

Applied Dental Biomaterials · 2026–2027

💡 ترجمة المصطلحات: جنب المصطلحات المهمة هتلاقي معناها بالعربي (بالشكل ده). أي مصطلح ترجمته ممكن تضيّع معناه سبناه بالإنجليزي زي ما هو.

📗 Part 1 — Introduction, requirements & classification

🕯️ Lost wax technique: steps after fabrication of the wax pattern
After the wax pattern is fabricated, the lost wax technique continues with three steps.
  • 1Investing: surrounding the wax pattern with a material that can accurately duplicate its shape and anatomic features.
  • 2Burnout (حرق الشمع): removal of the wax pattern so that a mold (القالب) is created into which the molten alloy can be placed.
  • 3Casting (السباكة): introduction of molten alloy into the mold.
Sequence shown in the slide diagram: pattern (on the die) → spruing (sprue base) → investing → burnout → casting. Key of the diagram: wax · investment · void · metal casting.
💍 Investment ring
Parts labelled in the slide diagram: casting ring (حلقة السباكة) · ring liner (بطانة الحلقة) · wax pattern (النموذج الشمعي) · sprue former · crucible former.
The diagram labels a minimum distance of 6 mm between the wax pattern and the end of the ring.
The second ring diagram also labels: 6 mm · 3 mm without liner · ceramic paper liner · metal ring · sprue pin.
🧱 What is an investment material?
An investment material is a ceramic material which is suitable for forming a mold (القالب) into which molten metal or alloy (السبيكة المنصهرة) is cast.
✅ Ideal requirements of investment materials
  • 1The mold must expand to compensate for the shrinkage on cooling of the alloy.
  • 2The set material should be permeable to allow air to escape as the molten alloy enters the mold.
  • 3High strength of the material to withstand the casting force of the molten alloy entering the mold.
  • 4The powder should be of fine particle size (حجم الحبيبات) to ensure a smooth surface on the casting.
  • 5The mixed unset material should have a smooth consistency (قوام ناعم).
  • 6The material should have a suitable setting time.
📐 Compensating for the dimensional changes
The desired accuracy of the casting is about 0.1%; therefore the lost wax procedure requires specially developed materials that compensate for the dimensional changes indicated by the following equation:
Shrinkage (wax + alloy) = Investment expansion
  • 1Wax shrinks on cooling.
  • 2Metal shows two types of shrinkage: (a) from melting to solidification temperature; (b) from solidification to room temperature.
🧪 General composition of investment materials
ComponentRole
1. Refractory substance (مادة مقاومة للحرارة)A material that will not decompose or disintegrate on heating.
2. Binder (المادة الرابطة)A material which will set and bind together the particles of the refractory substance.
🗂️ Types of investment materials
Classified according to the type of binder:
  • 1Gypsum-bonded investment.
  • 2Phosphate-bonded investment.
  • 3Silicate-bonded investment.

📙 Part 2 — Gypsum-bonded investment

🎯 1) Uses
  • 1Suitable for casting gold alloys.
  • 2Used when the casting temperature does not exceed 700°–1000 °C.
Note: above 700 °C a reaction occurs between calcium sulfate and silica (see Limitations below), and the burnout temperature for gold alloys with gypsum-bonded investment is 450–700 °C.
🧬 2) Composition
ComponentAmountPurpose
Refractory material (مادة مقاومة للحرارة): crystalline polymorphs of silica (quartz or cristobalite)about 55–75%Acts as a refractory material during heating of the investment · regulates thermal expansion
Binder (المادة الرابطة): CaSO4 hemihydrate (gypsum) (كبريتات الكالسيوم نصف المائية)about 25–45%Binds the refractory material to form a coherent mass · gives sufficient strength to the mold · contributes to the mold expansion by the setting expansion
Modifiers—Accelerators and retarders (regulate setting time and expansion) · reducing agents (عوامل مختزلة) such as powdered charcoal or copper to reduce any oxide formed in the metal during casting
In setting, the hemihydrate binder combines with water to form dihydrate (gypsum) (ثنائية الماء).
📏 3) Dimensional changes of the mold: overview
Four dimensional changes occur in gypsum-bonded investment:
  • 1Normal setting expansion
  • 2Hygroscopic expansion
  • 3Thermal expansion
  • 4Shrinkage on heating
📈 Normal setting expansion
  • 1It is the linear expansion that takes place during setting in air.
  • 2It is caused by the crystal outgrowth of gypsum (نمو البلورات).
  • 3Its purpose is to aid in enlarging the mold to compensate partially for the casting shrinkage.
💧 Hygroscopic expansion
Hygroscopic expansion (التمدد في وجود الماء) is a linear expansion that occurs if the investment is in contact with water during setting. The expansion may be more than double the setting expansion in air.
It can be achieved by one of the following:
  • 1Immersing the casting ring in a water bath immediately after investing the wax pattern.
  • 2Using the wet liner inside the casting ring.
  • 3Addition of water on the surface of the investment using a syringe.
🌡️ Thermal expansion
Thermal expansion (التمدد الحراري): investment materials containing cristobalite and quartz show rapid expansions at different temperature ranges.
Silica polymorphExpansionRapid expansion betweenα → β transition
Cristobalite1.6%200–300 °C200–270 °C
Quartz1.4%500–600 °C573 °C
Due to displacive transformation of the silica: the transformation from the α-form (low form) of silica to the β-form (high form), which is associated with thermal expansion.
  • 1As the α-form changes to the β-form when heated, the density decreases, resulting in an increase in volume, which leads to an increase in the linear expansion.
📉 Shrinkage on heating
  • 1This may be due to the dehydration of gypsum (فقدان الماء).
  • 2CaSO4 dihydrate → CaSO4 hemihydrate at 100–200 °C.
  • 3Above 200 °C, further dehydration gives the anhydrous form of CaSO4 (لامائية).
How to minimize this effect? This shrinkage is eliminated by a small quantity of additives such as NaCl.
🧷 Other properties
  • 1The total expansion of the mold is generally sufficient to compensate for the shrinkage on cooling of gold alloys.
  • 2The set investment is porous (المسامية); this helps to prevent porosity in castings.
  • 3The strength of these materials when they set is sufficient to withstand the forces of the molten alloy as it enters the mold.
⚠️ Limitations
Above 700 °C, a reaction can occur between calcium sulfate and silica: CaSO4 + SiO2 → CaSiO3 + SO3
  • 1The sulfur trioxide gas that is evolved causes porosity and corrosion in the casting.
  • 2For this reason, gypsum-bonded investments are not used for the higher fusing dental alloys, such as cobalt-chromium.
  • 3In this case, phosphate-bonded or silicate-bonded materials are chosen.

📕 Part 3 — Phosphate-bonded investment

🎯 1) Uses
Used for casting alloys with a high melting temperature (1200 °C), such as base metal alloys used for ceramo-metal restorations (الترميمات الخزفية المعدنية).
🧬 2) Composition
ComponentDetails
1. BinderMagnesium oxide (basic) and ammonium dihydrogen phosphate.
2. Refractory materialSilica. Most phosphate-bonded investments are mixed with: (a) a specially prepared suspension of colloidal silica in water (سيليكا غروية), which increases expansion; or (b) water alone.
3. ModifiersSuch as carbon as a reducing agent (عامل مختزل).
⚗️ 3) Setting reaction
MgO (base) + NH4H2PO4 (acid) → MgNH4PO4 + H2O (in an aqueous system)
  • 1The crystals of magnesium ammonium phosphate bind together the particles of the silica refractory.
  • 2Because of the high strength of the set material, metal casting rings are not needed.
  • 3Plastic rings are used until the material sets, then removed before heating the investment.
📈 4) Properties: expansion
  • 1The setting reaction is accompanied by an expansion, similar to the crystal growth of gypsum.
  • 2Thermal expansion occurs on heating.
N.B.: mixing with water gives less thermal expansion than mixing with colloidal silica solution.
🕳️ 5) Properties: porosity
  • 1The set phosphate-bonded material shows sufficient porosity (المسامية) to allow the escape of gases during the casting procedure.
💪 6) Properties: strength
  • 1The strength is more than that of gypsum investment.
  • 2The set material increases in strength during heating.
Reason: chemical interaction between silica and the binder, giving complex silicophosphates.
📏 Distance from the end of the ring
The minimum distance between the wax pattern and the end of the ring (6 mm) is reduced to 4 mm in the case of phosphate-bonded investment.
The slide asks “Why?” but the answer is not written on it, so ask your doctor for the reason.
⚖️ Advantages and disadvantages
AdvantagesDisadvantages
High strength at room temperature and at high temperature
High setting and thermal expansion		Show breakdown above 1400 °C
Difficult divesting (إخراج السباكة من القالب)

📘 Part 4 — Silicate-bonded investment

🎯 1) Uses
  • 1Used for alloys with very high melting temperatures (up to 1400 °C).
  • 2Mainly for casting alloys for partial denture bases (الأطقم الجزئية).
🧬 2) Composition
PartContents
PowderRefractory particles of silica and magnesium oxide.
LiquidSupplied as two different liquids: one contains diluted water-soluble ethyl silicate solution; the second contains diluted hydrochloric acid (HCl).
⚗️ 3) Setting reaction: Stage 1 — Hydrolysis
  • 1The two liquids are mixed in equal volumes.
  • 2Ethyl silicate is hydrolysed (التحلل المائي) to silicic acid, with liberation of ethyl alcohol.
Ethyl silicate + hydrochloric acid + water → polysilicic acid + ethyl alcohol
⚗️ Stage 2 — Gelation
  • 1The sol of silicic acid and ethyl alcohol is mixed with the powder (cristobalite or quartz and magnesium oxide).
  • 2Magnesium oxide is used to give alkaline conditions leading to gel formation.
  • 3There is a slight shrinkage at this stage.
⚗️ Stage 3 — Drying
  • 1On heating, considerable shrinkage occurs and there is a loss of alcohol and water, leaving a mold made of silica particles tightly packed together.
  • 2Shrinkage of the gel takes place (green shrinkage).
📈 4) Properties: dimensional changes
  • 1There is a large amount of thermal expansion, due to the very large percentage of silica.
  • 2This expansion is usually sufficient to compensate for shrinkage.
🕳️ 5) Properties: porosity
  • 1The particles of the set material are packed so closely together that porosity is negligible.
  • 2Air spaces or vents (فتحات التهوية) must be left in the investment to permit escape of the air from the mold.
⚖️ Advantages and disadvantages
AdvantagesDisadvantages
Ability to cast high melting alloys
High thermal expansion
Can produce castings with fine details		High density and less porous (so a vent is needed)

📗 Part 5 — Casting technique

🛠️ Steps of the casting technique
  • 1Spruing (تركيب قنوات الصب)
  • 2Lining the ring with a liner
  • 3Mixing investment
  • 4Filling the ring
  • 5Wax burnout
  • 6Alloy and mold temperature
  • 7Casting
  • 8Finishing and polishing
🔥 Wax burnout
  • 1The sprue base and former are removed and the wax pattern is burnt out to leave the completed mold cavity.
  • 2The choice of investment material depends on the type of alloy which is to be cast.
  • 3The temperature to which the investment mold is heated during burnout controls the thermal expansion of the investment.
AlloyBurnout temperature
Gold alloys (with gypsum-bonded investments)450–700 °C (commonly used)
Ni/Cr alloys700–900 °C
Co/Cr alloys1000 °C
✨ Finishing and polishing: noble alloys
Noble alloys (السبائك النبيلة): after the casting has been completed, the ring is removed and quenched in water (التبريد السريع بالماء), which gives two advantages.
  • 1The noble alloy is left in a soft condition for burnishing and polishing.
  • 2When the water contacts the hot investment, an aggressive reaction occurs: the investment becomes friable and soft (سهل التفتت) and the casting is more easily cleaned.
The surface of the casting appears dark with oxides and tarnish (تعتّم), which can be removed by pickling.
Pickling (التنظيف بالحمض) is heating the discolored casting in an acid such as 50% HCl or sulfuric acid solution.
🔩 Finishing and polishing: base metal alloys
Base metal alloys (السبائك غير النبيلة):
  • 1They are bench cooled to room temperature before the casting is removed from the investment.
  • 2Castings from these alloys are generally not pickled.

⭐ Key Terms & Facts

أهم المصطلحات والمعلومات في المحاضرة — 25 نقطة للمراجعة قبل الامتحان.
1
Lost wax technique
After the wax pattern is fabricated: 1) investing (surrounding the pattern with a material that accurately duplicates its shape and anatomic features), 2) burnout (removal of the wax pattern so a mold is created) and 3) casting (introduction of molten alloy into the mold).
تقنية الشمع المفقود
2
Investment material
A ceramic material suitable for forming a mold into which molten metal or alloy is cast. The ring parts are the casting ring, ring liner, wax pattern, sprue former and crucible former, with a minimum distance of 6 mm between the pattern and the ring end.
تعريف مادة الـ Investment
3
Ideal requirements
Expands to compensate for alloy shrinkage · permeable so air escapes · high strength against the casting force · fine particle size for a smooth casting surface · smooth consistency when mixed · suitable setting time.
المتطلبات المثالية
4
Dimensional compensation
Desired casting accuracy is about 0.1%. Shrinkage (wax + alloy) = investment expansion. Wax shrinks on cooling; metal shrinks from melting to solidification temperature and from solidification to room temperature.
تعويض التغيرات البعدية
5
General composition
Refractory substance: will not decompose or disintegrate on heating. Binder: sets and binds together the particles of the refractory substance.
مكونات مادة الـ Investment
6
Types of investment
Classified by binder: gypsum-bonded, phosphate-bonded and silicate-bonded.
أنواع مواد الـ Investment
7
Gypsum-bonded: uses and composition
Used for casting gold alloys. Composition: quartz or cristobalite (about 55–75%, refractory and regulates thermal expansion), CaSO4 hemihydrate (about 25–45%, binds, gives strength and setting expansion) and modifiers (accelerators, retarders, charcoal or copper as reducing agents).
الجبسي: الاستخدام والتركيب
8
Four dimensional changes
Gypsum-bonded investment shows four: normal setting expansion, hygroscopic expansion, thermal expansion and shrinkage on heating.
التغيرات البعدية الأربعة
9
Normal setting expansion
Linear expansion during setting in air, caused by the crystal outgrowth of gypsum. It enlarges the mold to compensate partially for the casting shrinkage.
التمدد أثناء التصلب
10
Hygroscopic expansion
Linear expansion when the investment is in contact with water during setting; may be more than double the setting expansion in air. Achieved by a water bath right after investing, a wet liner, or adding water on the surface with a syringe.
التمدد في وجود الماء
11
Thermal expansion
Cristobalite (1.6%) expands rapidly at 200–300 °C and quartz (1.4%) at 500–600 °C, due to the displacive transformation of silica from the α-form to the β-form. On heating the density decreases and the volume increases.
التمدد الحراري
12
Shrinkage on heating
Due to dehydration of gypsum: dihydrate → hemihydrate at 100–200 °C, then the anhydrous form above 200 °C. It is eliminated by a small quantity of additives such as NaCl.
الانكماش عند التسخين
13
Gypsum-bonded: other properties
Total expansion is sufficient to compensate for the shrinkage of gold alloys. The set investment is porous (helps prevent porosity in castings) and its strength withstands the forces of the molten alloy.
خواص أخرى
14
Gypsum-bonded: limitations
Above 700 °C: CaSO4 + SiO2 → CaSiO3 + SO3. The sulfur trioxide gas causes porosity and corrosion in the casting, so it is not used for high fusing alloys such as cobalt-chromium; phosphate-bonded or silicate-bonded materials are chosen.
عيوب الجبسي
15
Phosphate-bonded: uses and composition
Used for alloys with a high melting temperature (1200 °C), such as base metal alloys for ceramo-metal restorations. Binder: magnesium oxide (basic) and ammonium dihydrogen phosphate; refractory: silica, mixed with colloidal silica solution (increases expansion) or with water alone; modifier: carbon (reducing agent).
الفوسفاتي: الاستخدام والتركيب
16
Phosphate-bonded: reaction
MgO (base) + NH4H2PO4 (acid) → MgNH4PO4 + H2O. The crystals bind the silica particles. Because of the high strength, metal casting rings are not needed; plastic rings are used until setting, then removed before heating.
تفاعل الفوسفاتي
17
Phosphate-bonded: properties
Setting expansion (like gypsum crystal growth) plus thermal expansion; mixing with water gives less thermal expansion than colloidal silica. Sufficient porosity for gases to escape. Strength greater than gypsum and it increases on heating due to complex silicophosphates formed between silica and the binder.
خواص الفوسفاتي
18
Phosphate-bonded: ring distance, pros and cons
The 6 mm distance to the ring end is reduced to 4 mm. Advantages: high strength at room and high temperature, high setting and thermal expansion. Disadvantages: breakdown above 1400 °C, difficult divesting, more dense and less porous than gypsum-bonded.
مميزات وعيوب الفوسفاتي
19
Silicate-bonded: uses and composition
For alloys with very high melting temperatures (up to 1400 °C), mainly partial denture bases. Powder: silica and magnesium oxide. Liquid: two liquids, one with diluted water-soluble ethyl silicate and one with diluted hydrochloric acid.
السيليكاتي: الاستخدام والتركيب
20
Silicate-bonded: reaction
Stage 1 — Hydrolysis: liquids mixed in equal volumes; ethyl silicate + HCl + water → polysilicic acid + ethyl alcohol. Stage 2 — Gelation: the sol is mixed with the powder; MgO gives alkaline conditions leading to gel formation (slight shrinkage). Stage 3 — Drying: on heating, considerable shrinkage with loss of alcohol and water (green shrinkage), leaving packed silica particles.
تفاعل السيليكاتي
21
Silicate-bonded: properties, pros and cons
Large thermal expansion (very large percentage of silica), usually sufficient to compensate for shrinkage. Porosity is negligible, so vents must be left for air to escape. Advantages: casts high melting alloys, high thermal expansion, fine details. Disadvantage: high density and less porous.
خواص ومميزات السيليكاتي
22
Casting technique
1) Spruing · 2) Lining the ring with a liner · 3) Mixing investment · 4) Filling the ring · 5) Wax burnout · 6) Alloy and mold temperature · 7) Casting · 8) Finishing and polishing.
خطوات السباكة
23
Wax burnout
The choice of investment depends on the type of alloy. The burnout temperature controls the thermal expansion of the investment: gold alloys 450–700 °C (with gypsum-bonded), Ni/Cr 700–900 °C, Co/Cr 1000 °C.
حرق الشمع
24
Finishing: noble alloys
The ring is removed and quenched in water: the alloy is left soft for burnishing and polishing, and the hot investment reacts with water and becomes friable and soft, so the casting is more easily cleaned. Dark oxides and tarnish are removed by pickling (heating in an acid such as 50% HCl or sulfuric acid).
السبائك النبيلة
25
Finishing: base metal alloys
Bench cooled to room temperature before the casting is removed from the investment, and generally not pickled.
السبائك غير النبيلة

Normal setting expansionLinear expansion during setting in air · crystal outgrowth of gypsum · partially compensates casting shrinkage
Hygroscopic expansionLinear expansion when in contact with water during setting · may be more than double the setting expansion in air
Hygroscopic methodsWater bath right after investing · wet liner inside the ring · water added on the surface with a syringe
Thermal expansionCristobalite 1.6% (rapid at 200–300 °C) · quartz 1.4% (rapid at 500–600 °C)
Displacive transformationα-form to β-form of silica · quartz at 573 °C · cristobalite at 200–270 °C · density decreases, volume increases
Shrinkage on heatingDehydration of gypsum · dihydrate to hemihydrate at 100–200 °C · eliminated by a small quantity of NaCl
Other propertiesTotal expansion sufficient for gold alloys · porous (prevents porosity in castings) · strength sufficient for the molten alloy
LimitationsAbove 700 °C: CaSO4 + SiO2 → CaSiO3 + SO3 · porosity and corrosion · not used for Co-Cr · phosphate-bonded or silicate-bonded chosen

🟪 Phosphate-bonded

الـ Investment المرتبط بالفوسفات

UsesHigh melting alloys (1200 °C) such as base metal alloys for ceramo-metal restorations
BinderMagnesium oxide (basic) and ammonium dihydrogen phosphate
Refractory materialSilica · mixed with colloidal silica suspension (increases expansion) or with water alone
ModifiersCarbon as a reducing agent
ReactionMgO (base) + NH4H2PO4 (acid) → MgNH4PO4 + H2O · crystals bind the silica particles
Casting ringsMetal rings not needed because of high strength · plastic rings used until setting, then removed before heating
ExpansionSetting expansion (like gypsum crystal growth) · thermal expansion on heating · water gives less thermal expansion than colloidal silica
PorositySufficient porosity to allow escape of gases during casting
StrengthMore than gypsum · increases during heating · chemical interaction between silica and binder gives complex silicophosphates
Ring distanceThe 6 mm distance to the ring end is reduced to 4 mm
AdvantagesHigh strength at room and high temperature · high setting and thermal expansion
DisadvantagesBreakdown above 1400 °C · difficult divesting · more dense and less porous than gypsum-bonded

🟩 Silicate-bonded

الـ Investment المرتبط بالسيليكات

UsesAlloys with very high melting temperatures (up to 1400 °C) · mainly partial denture bases
PowderRefractory particles of silica and magnesium oxide
LiquidsTwo liquids: diluted water-soluble ethyl silicate solution · diluted hydrochloric acid (HCl)
Stage 1: HydrolysisLiquids mixed in equal volumes · ethyl silicate + HCl + water → polysilicic acid + ethyl alcohol
Stage 2: GelationSol mixed with the powder · MgO gives alkaline conditions leading to gel formation · slight shrinkage
Stage 3: DryingOn heating: considerable shrinkage, loss of alcohol and water, packed silica particles · shrinkage of the gel (green shrinkage)
Dimensional changesLarge thermal expansion because of the very large percentage of silica · usually sufficient to compensate for shrinkage
Porosity and ventsPorosity negligible · vents must be left to permit escape of air from the mold
AdvantagesCasts high melting alloys · high thermal expansion · fine details
DisadvantagesHigh density and less porous (needs a vent)

🔥 Casting technique

خطوات السباكة

StepsSpruing · lining the ring with a liner · mixing investment · filling the ring · wax burnout · alloy and mold temperature · casting · finishing and polishing
Wax burnoutSprue base and former removed · pattern burnt out · choice of investment depends on the type of alloy
Burnout temperatureControls the thermal expansion of the investment
Gold alloys450–700 °C with gypsum-bonded investments
Ni/Cr alloys700–900 °C
Co/Cr alloys1000 °C
Noble alloysRing removed and quenched in water · alloy left soft for burnishing and polishing · hot investment becomes friable and soft, easier cleaning
PicklingHeating the discolored casting in an acid such as 50% HCl or sulfuric acid solution · removes dark oxides and tarnish
Base metal alloysBench cooled to room temperature before removal from the investment · generally not pickled
